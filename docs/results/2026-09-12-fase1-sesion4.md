## 2.1 The anchor names no floor the window can keep

At eight rows unfolded, the five facts uncut need a body row of **561 px** (batten 318.9 × 38.0
pinned, 302.3 × 38.0 in the rail); clear of the corner mark's band, 634; the gap's arrowhead plus 6
px of line, **≈ 1 300**. The shipped window gives **534**. Nothing on #81's table is under the
floor at any depth — at 382 px the drawing holds its five facts nowhere, including the one-row
stacked node that 64 of the 88 are — and the facts wrap to two lines in every case at 302 px. Both
are criteria the fold resolves, so clamping the window at either would scroll the app at rest on the
machine it is designed for to protect a drawing #82 never draws. That was the finding, not a failure
to take the reading.

What the anchor bought is what it was asked to buy, asserted rather than assumed: a card's height is
decided by the row count and by nothing else — not the patch, not the parking, not the class — and
never grows with it (`wide-layout.spec.ts`); with the switch on no node gets a taller card than with
it off, over both wide classes, forced by number (`operator-diagram.spec.ts`). So the 66 is the worst
case, the fold can only buy margin, and the threshold derived at the floor cannot spiral. The
dependency loop #73's grilling named — floor from the deepest drawing that still says what it says,
threshold from the floor — is cut by measuring the floor on the *unfolded* drawing, which is what the
bench's fold-off switch exists for (#79).

## 2.2 The legend was eating eight pixels, and they were deciding the threshold

`.legend__swatch` is declared 22 × 14 with a `--rule-min` border and nothing sets `box-sizing:
border-box`, so the swatch renders **26 × 18**, a row is 18 and the band is **62 px** against the 54
`legend.ts` computed. `LEGEND_H` enters the floor chain twice — once raising `BODY_FLOOR`, once
subtracted at `floorCanvasHeight()` — so the drawing sees only the difference: the floor is 382 and
the canvas at the floor is 282, restoring the relation the floor is written as. The build won.

At the floor (canvas 282, y-scale 0.705), per gap and uniform across each drawing:

| rows | gap | arrowhead | visible line | clears 6 px |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 (algorithm 55) | 12.41 px | 6.35 px | **6.06 px** | by **0.06** |
| 6 (algorithm 37) | 10.34 | 6.35 | 4.00 | no |
| 8 (algorithm 66) | 7.77 | 6.35 | 1.42 | no |

So the depth trigger is `rows ≥ 6` and folds **exactly {37, 66}** against the histogram read off the
drawing — `1×1, 2×26, 3×37, 4×17, 5×5, 6×1, 8×1` — which is where the proposal's arithmetic put it.
**The margin is 0.06 px and not the 0.15 the proposal recorded** (at a 286 px canvas), and at the
278 px canvas the screen was drawing before #81 it was **−0.02**: the same gap left 5.98 px, the bin
