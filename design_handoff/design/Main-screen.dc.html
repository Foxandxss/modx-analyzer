WHY IT IS A MODE AND NOT A SCREEN
Your argument for its own screen was room for two captures. It falls if the two go superimposed instead of side by side — and superimposed reads better, because the difference jumps out instead of having to be hunted for. At 1280 px, two spectra side by side would be 183 px each: illegible. On the same axes they are 700.

And comparing is what you do mientras you build: pulling the owner off his bench to look at two values is friction. A/B mode reuses the panels already there — the spectrum and the harmonics with Bessel — and only swaps the figures column for deltas.
DECISIONES DE LECTURA
A and B are told apart by fill, not by colour: A is a hollow, dimmed bar, B is solid and luminous. Dashed stays reserved for prediction — and here there are dos predictions, one per index.

The difference is labelled, not deduced: “the fundamental loses 14 dB”, “the 9th gains 31 dB”. And the column says lo que no cambia — fc, fm, ratio, note and velocity identical — because that is what makes the comparison valid.
