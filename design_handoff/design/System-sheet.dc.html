THE FOUR STATES THAT ARE NOT HAPPY
Keyboard not connected
El puerto MODX-1 is missing, or another app holds it exclusively. Audio is still coming in: you can look, you cannot read the patch.
DIAGRAM FROZEN · LAST POLL 4 s
Sin audio entrando
Not silence: exact zeroes, so there is no cable and no route. Almost always it is Part Output = USB1&2, which does not exist outside ASIO.
REAL SILENCE ≠ A CABLE IN THE WRONG PLACE
The write did not apply
The MODX fails silently: writing with the wrong length gives no error and changes nothing. Every write is verified by reading back, and here 1 de 3 no volvió.

INTERACCIÓN · DEDO PRIMERO, RATÓN COMO SUPERCONJUNTO
The app is used with the left hand on the MODX and the right on the screen. So the base design is touch and the mouse adds añade precision, never functionality. One design for both pointers: there is no “touch mode”.
The finger covers what it drags
The active point's value appears arriba y al lado, clear of the hand's shadow. Never directly under the point being dragged.

WHAT THE FINGER CAN DO (AND THEREFORE EVERYONE CAN)
Touching an operator edits it. Dragging on a knob moves it; on a curve, it moves the point. Touching a Spectral Form drawing selects it. Touching the bipolar track jumps to that value and dragging on refines it. Every action has its touchable route: no context menu and no hidden gesture is ever the only way.
· no :hover to discover, show or reveal
· sin clic derecho como vía única
· no double tap for anything destructive
· the value is always visible, or appears on touch
WHAT THE MOUSE ADDS, ON TOP
Wheel for ±1 on any knob, Alt for ×0.1 fine steps, double click to return to the default, keyboard shortcuts for CAPTURE and for switching views, and a context menu with the same things already on screen. All of it is capa adicional: removing the mouse removes no function.
DPR 1.5 · CONSECUENCIA DE DIBUJO
Half pixels make 1 px hairlines dirty: rules at 2 px or separation by colour and space. Signal, diagram, curves and grids are vector or DPR-scaled canvas — cero bitmaps de tamaño fijo.
