Round 7 — the app can know where your hands are
Dos piezas de comportamiento, sin pantalla nueva: following lives in the editor's rail (7a) and in the ALL EIGHT cards (7b). The polling that already exists is all it takes — if, in one round, 49 30 1A moves, the app does not know you are looking at OP4: it knows you just touched something on OP4, que es mejor.
7a The rail that follows you — a visible, reversible suggestion, not a jump cabecera de 5a · 1280×58 + the suggestion band
Elijo sugerir and not jumping, even with the rail of the eight one touch away. The reason is not reversibility —the rail already gives that— it is that dragging an envelope point cannot be interrupted: if focus jumps while your finger is on OP3's curve, the gesture breaks mid-curve. A suggestion waits; a jump does not.

A change that coincides with the app's own write pendiente de releer is not yours: it is the pendiente → releída → confirmada cycle doing its job. When the tutor writes three of OP5's parameters, focus no se mueve and the notice that appears is the usual one: WRITTEN AND VERIFIED.
AND THE SUGGESTION CLEARS ITSELF
A los 6 s with no further activity there, the band goes. What it does no not do is accumulate: touch five things on five operators and you do not get five bands — you get the last one, and the rail marks all five. A trace is useful; a queue of notices is not.

YOUR HANDS IN PHOSPHOR, THE TUTOR IN CYAN
A change's provenance uses the vocabulary that already exists: phosphor for what comes from outside (your hands, like every measured signal), cyan for what the app wrote. Not one new colour, and it reads without a legend.
