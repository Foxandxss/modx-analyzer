THE INTERESTING DECISION: CONTEXT
You were right that the problem was there. I discarded two options: the full eight-node diagram no cabe beside two envelopes, and a breadcrumb (Part 1 › OP3) loses exactly what matters — the topology.

Lo que hay es your branch, and only your branch: who modulates you, you, and who you modulate, with the real algorithm’s arrows. Plus the rail of the eight in the header, which is context and navigation at once: at a glance you see who is a carrier, who is a modulator and who is at zero, and you jump with a 32 px touch.
JERARQUÍA SIN PESTAÑAS
What gets touched every minute takes 78 % of the height: Level as a 190 px knob, the dos envolventes a lo ancho with 26 px points, and the spectral form as seven drawings.

What is set once lives in a franja inferior de 172 px, with its label saying so: Level Scaling over the keyboard, the bipolar Detune, and four chips for Time/Key, Lvl/Vel, Pitch/Vel and Key On Reset. Present and touchable, small because they are — zero tabs, and not one list of etiqueta: campo anywhere on the screen.

Skirt aparece apagado, no ausente: the form is Odd 1 and only Res 1 / Res 2 enable it. And it is eight bars, because the range is 0–7.

YOUR INSTINCT WAS THE RIGHT ONE, AND I TAKE IT ONE STEP FURTHER
El eje es la trayectoria por armónico, not the spectrum per step. And the consequence that makes it whole: those trajectories do not resemble the Bessel functions, they are the Bessel functions — so the prediction is not drawn “beside” them, it is overlaid on the same curve and the eye compares without moving.

Eight spectra cannot be read at once, and I do not try: there is a draggable vertical line that pulls one step's spectrum into the right-hand panel. You go from the map to the detail when you need to, not always.

Y el regalo: en Level ≈ 77 la fundamental se anula — the first zero of J₀ at I ≈ 2.405. You watch it cross the axis. No single spectrum teaches that.
AND HERE I ARGUE WITH A NUMBER OF YOURS
Dices «son minutos, no segundos». The spikes' arithmetic gives ~25 s. Per step: writing is 0.03 s and verifying a parameter by reading back is 2 ms; hold 1.4 s; a 65536 window at 44 100 Hz is 1,486 s; the release tail 0.46 s; and the pause 0.4 s. That is ~3.1 s × 8 ≈ 25 s.

It only rises to minutes if several captures are averaged per step, which probably quieras near the zeros — which is exactly where the noise floor rules and where the worst deviation is. So the progress display is designed to hold both scales: eight cards with the sub-phase inside, and the running one widens. If it ends up a minute per step, the design does not change; only the “left” figure does.

WHY A DRAWER AND NOT A SCREEN
It is the same argument that won A/B: you have to see it while it happens. If I take you to another screen to watch the verification, you can no longer see the cause — the knob you just turned. The drawer opens over the bottom strip and the screen behind stays alive.
I BUY THE REASON, NOT THE PANEL
Your argument is the right one and it is not about debugging: this app's entire discipline is verifying by reading back, and there was nowhere to watch that verification happen. A rule you cannot watch is a promise.
HOW I KEEP IT FROM BEING A DEBUG PANEL
Por defecto no es un log: abre mostrando sólo the unconfirmed writes — two actionable lines, each with its button. Full traffic is one touch away, but never the first thing.

And the header's chip only calls for attention when there is something: neutral when nothing is pending, in alert with the count when there is.
LO QUE NO LLEVA
No messages-per-second counter, no latency graph, no live dump scrolling by itself. Three stable figures (median, p99, lost) and that is all: nothing moving in competition with the signal.

Nor is it the home of Receive Bulk: that is a failure with a consequence and lives in 4e. Here, only what can be repaired with one touch.
