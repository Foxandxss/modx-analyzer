WHERE IT LIVES · THE FAR RIGHT OF EVERY HEADER
MODX-1 · MAIN L/R
LIVE · 30 fps CAPTURE
Isolation instead of confirmation. Un filete de 2 px y 16 px of air separate it from everything — twice the normal --hit-gap . Nothing actionable to its left within that strip, and it is the last element: to its right is the window edge.
It acts on finger-up inside. Touch it by accident and drag off before releasing and nothing happens. It is the one concession, and it costs no time in an emergency because the natural gesture is touch and lift.
LA TENSIÓN, RESUELTA
You asked me to decide and explain it: large, unmistakable and with no confirmation. An “are you sure?” turns an emergency into two steps, and whoever presses it already knows what they want.

What makes that decision safe is not a guard, it is that the cost of pressing it by mistake is low: silence, and nothing else.

Nothing to undo, because nothing was undone. The acknowledgement lasts 1.5 s and clears itself: it does not ask for a second touch to close.
Sobrevive al estado de error. It does not live inside any panel a failure screen could replace: the header is the last thing replaced, and the four unhappy-state cards are drawn debajo it, never over it. If the keyboard disconnects, the button is still there and still sends the messages in case the port comes back.
It does not belong to the player. You were right to promote it: the stuck notes in phase 0c were not left by anyone playing, they were left by a bug in the code that generated them. A button depending on the player would have been tan roto como él.

I ACCEPT THE QUALIFICATION
You were right: the check is not only a sequence. The resting face ya existía and it was the header's chip. What I have done is give it a second state — when something fails, the chip says the consequence and not the fault (“I can’t give you your patch back”, not “Receive Bulk in Protect”), and touching it opens this screen.

And I have refused to put in a permanent panel with six lights: seis verdes ocupando sitio no informan de nada.
POR QUÉ ESTE ORDEN
Most serious to least, and the one that fails stays al final y grande, with the full menu path inside. The five that pass collapse to a line with their measured figure — they are there to be watched going by, not to be read.

The header does not say “5 of 6 OK”: it says what you are not going to be able to do. A counter is a datum; a consequence is information.
